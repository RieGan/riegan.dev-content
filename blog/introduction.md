# Introduction

## About Me

Hi, I am Ganjar Muhammad Parikesit, also known as `riegan`.

I am a Fullstack Software Engineer based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia.

I work across frontend, backend, tooling, and product systems. I write interface code, backend services, APIs, data flows, and the glue that keeps product workflows maintainable.

My current stack includes React, React Native, TypeScript, Rust, Fastify, tRPC, Python, Go, MySQL, Postgres, and Tailwind CSS.

I am open to software engineering projects and consultation. That can include application development, architecture review, implementation planning, platform work, internal tooling, or software security consultation when the work needs that perspective.

## Journey

### 2013: First Internet Experiments

My first real contact with development started in 2013, during junior high school. I was experimenting with free domains and free hosting to create a WordPress blog page. Around the same time, I also tried setting up a custom email address. It was simple, messy, and limited, but it made the internet feel buildable.

That first blog was not a serious product, but it was the start of the pattern I still enjoy: understand the tools, make something run, then adjust it until it feels useful. I was learning by touching the whole thing at once: domain, hosting, WordPress, email, pages, content, and the small operational details around them.

### Senior High School: Robotics and Programming

After that, I spent more time on robotics and competitive programming until 2017, during senior high school. Most of that came through robotics extracurricular activities and a science club focused on Olimpiade Sains Nasional preparation. I joined robotics competitions and qualified for OSN Computer Science at the provincial level. Robotics made software feel connected to the physical world: sensors, motors, timing, failures, and constraints. Competitive programming trained a different side of the same habit: break the problem down, reason carefully, and make the implementation fit the limits.

### College: Cybersecurity and Computer Science

During college at Universitas Gadjah Mada, my focus shifted toward cybersecurity. I joined competitions with my team and won several cybersecurity and Capture the Flag events, including Gemastik and multiple CTF competitions from 2019 to 2021. Those years shaped how I approach debugging, edge cases, implementation details, and technical tradeoffs.

I studied Computer Science at UGM from 2017 to 2024. My thesis focused on detecting low-and-slow DDoS attacks at the application layer using deep learning. Outside coursework, I worked as a Computer Lab Assistant and served in OmahTI UGM, including as Vice of Training Department. I also helped with community activities as a speaker, problem setter, coordinator, and event coordinator.

## Professional Work

### Janaprotrack

From February 2020 to February 2021, I worked on Janaprotrack, mainly on frontend implementation, backend API design, and API architecture. Janaprotrack is a professional tracker for Universitas Janabadra, built around student portfolio data for Surat Keterangan Pendamping Ijazah. The public app shows workflows for student portfolio input, evidence upload, verification, SKPI submission, SKPI preview and download, dashboards, statistics, rubric management, program and faculty management, readmission, and account migration.

### Ruangguru

My professional work at Ruangguru started with the gamification system on roboguru.com, then expanded into education platforms, AI products, sales tooling, and CRM systems. I helped Skill Academy revamp its app to React Native with a new UI, and my work also included Roboguru and Airis. On Airis, I worked on the core chat and animation system as part of the Ruangguru team. I also received a CHIEF Award during my Ruangguru work. Other work involved internal platforms and operational tools used by education, sales, and CRM teams.

The work ranged from frontend implementation to backend code, workflow design, API contracts, internal tooling, performance fixes, and deployment support. It also included finding and fixing multiple internal vulnerability issues before they were exposed, while keeping the details private. Some internal systems were especially end-to-end, covering repeated operational workflows, data import, assignment, communication flows, and CRM activity integration.

My current major work is an internal CRM platform rebuild. I initiated and became the lead architect and main contributor for a modern polyglot monorepo using React, Rust, Fastify, tRPC, Drizzle, MySQL, Postgres, and Apache Doris. The migration keeps old and new systems running side by side while improving the workflows available to the business.

## Current Focus

Across these projects, I have usually worked close to both product needs and implementation details. I write frontend code, backend code, API contracts, service glue, Dockerfiles, Kubernetes manifests, data flows, review notes, and migration adjustments. I also spend a lot of time reviewing code, pairing with teammates, reducing brittle abstractions, and turning unclear requirements into something shippable.

This site is where I keep selected notes, project references, and updates about what I am building.
